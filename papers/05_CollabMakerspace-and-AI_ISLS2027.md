# Decentralised Collaboration and AI-Mediated Practices in a Member-Governed Makerspace / 成员自治创客空间中的去中心化协作与 AI 中介实践

**Venue / 投稿**: ISLS 2027 short paper · **Status / 状态**: submitted short-paper version<br>
**Method / 方法**: interpretivist qualitative single-site case study; nine participants; abductive reflexive thematic analysis<br>
**Original paper / 原文**: [Download the submitted PDF / 下载原文 PDF](05_CollabMakerspace-and-AI_ISLS2027.pdf)

## The research question / 研究问题

How do makers describe the collaborative practices associated with a member-governed makerspace’s long-term survival, and how do AI tools support, complicate or threaten those practices?

成员如何描述一个由成员治理的创客空间得以长期持续的协作实践？AI 工具又如何支持、复杂化或威胁这些实践？

The case is a UK makerspace organised through member participation rather than a conventional management hierarchy. The paper treats sustainability as a learning-and-community question: what makes people return, contribute, trust one another and share responsibility?

研究对象是一个依靠成员参与、没有传统管理层级的英国创客空间。论文把持续性视为学习与社区问题：是什么让人们愿意回来、贡献、彼此信任并共同承担责任？

## Three findings / 三项发现

### 1. Reputational authority beneath formal governance / 正式治理之下的声誉权威

Rules, trustees, monitoring and graduated responses created a skeleton for access and accountability. Members also relied on socially recognised judgement—described by one participant as **mana**—that was earned through visible contribution and reliable care. Formal roles could ratify this authority, but could not manufacture it.

规则、受托人、监督和分级回应为准入与问责提供了骨架。成员同时依赖一种通过可见贡献与可靠照料赢得的社会性判断力，一位参与者称之为 **mana**。正式职位可以确认这种权威，却不能凭空制造它。

### 2. Sustained participation through mutual witnessing / 通过相互见证维持参与

Members often worked on separate projects. Participation stayed social because projects were shown, noticed, discussed and used as reasons to ask for or offer help. **Mutual witnessing** names this pattern: individual making becomes a visible, shareable route into competence, recognition and belonging.

成员经常各自进行不同项目。参与之所以保持社会性，是因为项目会被展示、被注意、被讨论，并成为寻求或提供帮助的理由。**相互见证**描述了这一模式：个人制作被转化为进入能力、认可与归属的可见、可分享路径。

### 3. Selective absorption of AI / 对 AI 的选择性吸收

Participants accepted bounded help with search, logistics, coding, data processing and early prototypes. They resisted uses that replaced comprehension, verification or collective deliberation. The boundary was practical rather than ideological: AI may assist, but consequential judgement must remain traceable to a person who understands and checks the material.

成员接受 AI 在搜索、日常事务、编程、数据处理和早期原型中的有限帮助，但抵制替代理解、核验或集体讨论的用法。这条边界是实践性的，而不是意识形态性的：AI 可以辅助，但重要判断必须能追溯到真正理解并核验材料的人。

## Why this matters for Learning Infrastructure / 与 Learning Infrastructure 的连接

| Infrastructure layer / 基础设施层 | Makerspace example / 创客空间对应 |
| --- | --- |
| Learning context / 学习情境 | Voluntary, member-governed collaborative making / 自愿参与、成员治理的协作式制作 |
| Theory and constructs / 理论与构念 | Commons governance, self-determination theory, sociomateriality, situated learning / 公共资源治理、自我决定理论、社会物质性、情境学习 |
| Intervention / 教学干预 | Visible contribution, peer recognition, bounded AI and accountable verification / 可见贡献、同伴认可、有边界的 AI 与问责核验 |
| Product pattern / 产品模式 | Community protocol that makes roles, provenance and review visible / 让角色、来源与审查可见的社区协议 |
| Evidence and measurement / 证据与测量 | Participation pathways, access barriers, trust, responsibility and boundary negotiation / 参与路径、准入障碍、信任、责任与边界协商 |

The paper extends the idea of a commons toward **epistemic and reputational resources**. It asks not only who can access shared information, but who performs the interpretive work required to use it responsibly. This gives Learning Infrastructure a social design principle: systems should preserve accountable participation, not only optimise task completion.

论文把公共资源的讨论扩展到**认识性资源与声誉资源**：问题不只是“谁能获取共享信息”，还包括“谁承担了负责任地使用这些信息所需的解释工作”。这为 Learning Infrastructure 提供了一个社会性设计原则：系统应保留可问责的参与，而不只是优化任务完成。

## Evidence boundary / 证据边界

This is a qualitative, single-site study based on reported experiences and interpretations. It does not establish causal effects of AI, statistical generalisability or a universal makerspace model. The concepts are analytical resources for designing and asking better questions in new settings.

本研究是基于经验叙述与解释的单点质性研究，不证明 AI 的因果效果、统计普遍性或一个适用于所有创客空间的模式。这些概念的作用，是帮助我们在新情境中设计并提出更好的问题。
